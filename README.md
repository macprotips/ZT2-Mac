# ZT2 Launcher (Beta)

Play your own copy of Zoo Tycoon 2 on an Apple Silicon Mac.

## What you need

- An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later
- Your own copy of Zoo Tycoon 2
- [CrossOver](https://www.codeweavers.com/crossover), installed, or a Sikarugir wrapper you already use (not yet tested)

## Setup

1. Download ZT2 Launcher from [Releases](https://github.com/macprotips/ZT2-Mac/releases) and move it to Applications.
2. Open it. Drag your Zoo Tycoon 2 folder (the one with `zt.exe` in it) onto the Zoo Tycoon 2 box, and CrossOver onto the CrossOver or Sikarugir box.
3. Click Install. The launcher asks once to download Microsoft's DirectX files (about 96 MB). This is its only network access.
4. Click Play. The first time, the game shows its licence agreement and then closes. Click Play again.

## Mods and saves

Click the gear to open Settings.

- **Mods:** add `.z2f` files, `.zip` files or folders, and switch packs on or off.
- **Saved Zoos:** back up or restore your zoos.
- **General:** resolution, video memory, and Reset.

## Known issues

- Installing from a game installer or disc image is untested, and so are Sikarugir wrappers.
- Reset moves the whole install, including saved zoos and mods, to the Trash. Back up first.
